CIMA Box 2.8.2
==============

CIMA Box changes your voice in real time using ElevenLabs, and can translate what other people
say (for example in VRChat) into your language.

It has five pages, switched at the top of the window:

  My voice   Your microphone goes to ElevenLabs and comes back as a different voice, which is
             played on any output device (for example a virtual cable that Discord or VRChat
             uses as its microphone). It can also re-speak or translate what you say.
  Text       Type instead of talking: the voice reads it (as typed or translated), and the
             whole conversation, other people's words included, shows in one place.
  Listen     Captions of what other people say, translated into your language.
  VR         Shows those captions (and your own words, if you like) on a small floating panel
             inside your headset, in any SteamVR game.
  Features   Extras you can turn on one by one: screen translation, a voice reading captions
             in your ear, quick phrases, profiles and more.

The switch at the top right turns the page you're on on or off. The gear next to it opens
Settings: the app's language, your API keys and hotkeys.


YOUR KEYS, YOUR COSTS
---------------------

CIMA Box is free, and it runs on your own accounts: ElevenLabs, and DeepL, Claude, Gemini or
OpenAI if you add them. Whatever it uses is charged by those services to your keys. The app says
this once, after the setup (or after updating from a version before 2.8), and again any time from Settings >
About > "About costs and your keys".

- Voices, captions and translations use credits or tokens for as long as they're switched on.
  Switch off what you aren't using.
- Some settings use a lot more: Listen left running, Captions while they talk, Hear them in my
  ear, Best translations and AI screen reading.
- Set a spending limit in Settings > Accounts, and look at what's left at the bottom of the
  window now and then.
- If you share a key with friends, what they use counts on your account too.

The maker of CIMA Box isn't responsible for charges, used-up credits or tokens, or anything else
that comes from using it. You choose the settings, so you use it at your own risk.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

The first time you open CIMA Box, a short setup asks for your language and then walks you
through everything one screen at a time, with pictures of where to click: your keys
(ElevenLabs, then DeepL and Gemini, Claude or OpenAI), what you want to do, your voice and where it
goes, including VB-CABLE for games and Discord. If it asks you to restart your PC, it continues
where you left off. Settings > About > Run setup again shows it again.

Already set up and need a key later? In Settings > Accounts, click "How do I get this?" under
it for the same step-by-step guide.

Doing it by hand instead:

1. Put CIMABox.exe anywhere you like (Desktop, Documents...) and run it. Your settings save by
   themselves, so you can move or update the program any time without losing them.
2. Click the gear (top right) to open Settings, paste your ElevenLabs API key under Accounts and
   click "Load voices and models". (Until you do, the bottom of the window shows these steps.)
   Want the menus in another language? Pick it under App language in the same place.
3. Under Voice, pick a voice.
4. Under Input, pick your microphone. Under Output, pick where the new voice goes.
5. To use the voice in Discord, VRChat, OBS or a game: install a virtual cable (for example
   VB-Audio Virtual Cable), choose "CABLE Input" under "Send the new voice to", and choose
   "CABLE Output" as the microphone in the other program.
6. Turn the switch at the top right on and talk.

Don't share cimabox.json with anyone. It holds your API keys.


MY VOICE PAGE
-------------

The page goes left to right in the order the sound travels: Input, then Voice, then Output.

Input
  Microphone ............... The mic you talk into.
  Start talking by ......... Voice activation: records whenever you talk.
                             Push to talk: records only while you hold a key.
  Opens above .............. (Voice activation) How loud you must be to start recording. Put
                             the marker on the level meter just to the right of where it sits
                             when you're silent.
  Pause that ends a phrase . How long a pause sends what you said. Shorter = less delay, but
                             it may cut you off between words.
  Key ...................... (Push to talk) Click it, then press the key you want to use.
  While not holding the key  (Push to talk) Silent: people hear nothing until you hold the key,
                             then only the new voice. My real voice: people hear your own voice
                             normally, and a line said while holding the key comes out in the
                             new voice (or translated). Your mic goes quiet from the moment you
                             press until that line has finished playing, so the two never
                             overlap. Needs the voice changer switch on.
  Longest phrase ........... If you talk without pausing, it is sent after this many seconds.

Voice
  Mode
    Changer ................ Changes your voice and keeps your timing, emotion and accent.
                             Fastest and most natural. Best choice for most uses.
    Re-speak ............... Writes down what you said, then the voice reads it out in its own
                             delivery. Use it to hide your accent or delivery.
    Translate .............. Like Re-speak, but translated into another language first.
  Voice .................... The ElevenLabs voice to use. Type in the box to search.
  A voice for each language  (Translate) Picking another language (or dialect) brings back the
                             voice you last used with it.
  Model .................... The ElevenLabs model. v4 Turbo and Flash are the fastest. Only v3
                             and v4 speak all 91 languages and follow emotion tags.
  I speak .................. (Translate) Your language, or "Detect automatically".
  Translate to ............. (Translate) The language the voice speaks. Type to search.
  Dialect .................. (Translate) Which way of speaking it: for Spanish Mexico, Spain,
                             Argentina, Puerto Rico, Colombia, Dominican Republic...; for Arabic
                             Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Gulf, Iraq, Egypt, Levant, Morocco...; Brazil
                             or Portugal; Quebec; Swiss German; Kansai; Hinglish; and more.
                             "Other (type it)" takes anything, like ar-KW or "Rioplatense".
                             Needs Claude, Gemini or OpenAI (DeepL and Free use the standard
                             language).
                             The words come out in the dialect; the ACCENT comes from the voice,
                             so for the real thing pick a voice from that region.
  Translation service ...... Free (MyMemory: no sign-up, about 5,000 characters a day),
                             DeepL (better wording, needs a DeepL API key; the free DeepL plan
                             gives 500,000 characters a month), Claude, Gemini or OpenAI (see
                             below). Free and DeepL sound like a textbook. The AI ones say it the
                             way a native speaker actually talks, slang and casual speech
                             included, which is what makes it believable in a conversation.
    Claude / Gemini / OpenAI key  Your key from console.anthropic.com (Claude),
                             aistudio.google.com (Gemini) or platform.openai.com (OpenAI). Costs
                             a tiny fraction of a cent per phrase.
    DeepL API key (backup) . Optional. Used for a phrase when the AI is too slow or fails.
    How you talk ........... Polite (like with someone you just met in a lobby), Casual (a
                             friend you hang out with, the default), Very casual (your closest
                             friend: slang, swearing kept) or My own: describe it in your own
                             words. Saying who you're talking to works best ("like with my best
                             friend", "laid back and a bit sarcastic").
                             Casual and Very casual don't translate your sentence word for
                             word: they say what you mean the way a friend would, however
                             neatly you said it, and never use polite forms. If a line still
                             comes out stiff, look at the bottom strip: "(... too slow)" means
                             DeepL or Free did that one, and they only know one way to talk.
    Sound like... .......... Optional, for example "a guy in his 20s from Seoul". Region and age
                             change the slang a lot.
    Match how they talk to me  Also gives the AI the last few lines other people said (from the
                             Listen page), so it picks up their words. Your style stays; it only
                             leans a little toward theirs. Needs Listen on.
    Time limit ............. If the AI hasn't answered by then (2 seconds by default), that phrase
                             uses DeepL (if you added a key) or Free instead. With a dialect it
                             waits a little longer. The bottom strip shows which service did
                             each phrase.
    AI model ............... Leave as is unless you want a bigger, slower model.
  Gender (AI services) ..... Many languages change words for who speaks and who listens
                             (Spanish cansado / cansada, Arabic enta / enti).
    I am ................... Not set, A man, A woman or Nonbinary.
    Talking to ............. Auto, Don't know, A guy, A girl or A group. Auto guesses from the
                             voice of the last person the Listen page heard (so Listen must be
                             on). It's only a guess: voice changers and avatar voices fool it.
                             Switch it fast with a hotkey (Settings > Hotkeys) or, in VR, from
                             the CIMA Box tab in the SteamVR menu.
  Carry my emotion ......... (Re-speak and Translate) Listens to HOW you said things: stressed
                             words, drawn-out words, pauses, laughs, questions, and whether you
                             sounded excited, flat, quiet or were whispering or shouting. It
                             passes that on to the voice. Adds no delay. Works fully with v3
                             and v4; other models only get the punctuation.
    Subtle / Strong ........ Strong picks up smaller changes in your voice.
    Relearn my usual voice . It compares you to your normal voice, learned from your first few
                             phrases. Press this after changing mics, or if it guesses wrong.
  Fine-tune the voice ...... Click to unfold these. The defaults suit most voices.
  Stability ................ Higher = steadier, calmer delivery. Lower = more emotional and
                             varied (and less predictable). 0.4 to 0.6 sounds most natural.
  Similarity ............... How closely it sticks to the original voice. Very high values can
                             bring out artifacts; around 0.75 is a good start.
  Style .................... Exaggerates the voice's style. Keep it at 0 for natural speech.
  Speed .................... How fast the voice talks.
  Speaker boost ............ Makes it sound more like the original voice. Try it off if the
                             voice sounds harsh or processed.
  Remove background noise .. (Changer) Cleans up your mic before conversion. Useful with a noisy
                             room or headset mic; adds a little delay.
  Set clone strength ....... (Only with your own Professional Voice Clone on a v4 model) Higher
                             follows your training recordings more closely; lower smooths the
                             voice. Off = the value saved on the ElevenLabs website.
  Voice and model IDs ...... For pasting an ID by hand. Not needed normally.

Output
  Send the new voice to .... Where the new voice plays (usually the virtual cable).
    Volume ................. How loud the new voice is there (100% = as it comes, up to 200%).
                             Your passed-through mic isn't affected.
  Hear it yourself too ..... Also plays it on your headphones so you can hear what others hear.
    Volume ................. How loud it is in your headphones.
  Audio quality
    Standard ............... Lowest delay, slightly duller sound. Any ElevenLabs plan.
    High ................... Same quality as the ElevenLabs website. Needs Creator or above.
    Best ................... Highest quality. Needs Pro or above.
                             If your plan doesn't allow a quality, it switches down by itself
                             and tells you.
  Pass my mic through when off  When the switch at the top right is off, your real voice goes
                             to the output instead of silence. Discord or VRChat can then stay
                             on the virtual cable all the time.
  Match my mic ............. Makes the new voice sound like it came through your actual mic
                             (same tone and loudness), so switching between your real voice and
                             the new one, or between two mics, isn't noticeable. It needs a few
                             seconds of you talking to learn. If your mic sounds dull, lower
                             Amount or turn it off.
    Amount ................. How strongly it matches.
    Relearn my mic ......... Starts learning your mic again.
  Show in VRChat chatbox ... Shows your words above your avatar. Choose What I said, Spoken as
                             (the translation) or Both. Both puts the translation on top and
                             your own words under it in brackets; if the two don't fit in
                             VRChat's 144 characters, your own words are shortened, never the
                             translation. Turn on OSC in VRChat first (Action Menu
                             > Options > OSC > Enabled). In Changer mode this also transcribes
                             each phrase, which ElevenLabs bills.
  Rescan devices ........... Refreshes the device lists after plugging something in.

Bottom strip
  Shows what it heard ("You said"), what the voice was told to say ("Spoken as"), how the
  phrase sounded (with Carry my emotion on), and how long each step took.
  Stop speaking ............ Cancels everything that is playing or waiting.


TEXT PAGE
---------

For when you can't or don't want to talk. Type at the bottom and press Enter (or Send).

  As typed / Translate ..... Read your words as they are, or translate them first. The voice,
                             model, languages, dialect, translation service and chatbox are the
                             same settings as on My voice.
  I type in ................ The language you write in (Detect automatically works too).
  Say it out loud .......... On: the voice says it through the output picked on My voice, so
                             people in the game or call hear it. Off: it only goes to the
                             conversation and the VRChat chatbox (if that is on).
  Conversation ............. Everything said, oldest at the top: your lines in amber (spoken or
                             typed), other people's in the color of their voice, translated, with
                             the original underneath. Other people's words come from Listen, so
                             press Start listening (or the switch at the top right) and pick what
                             to listen to on the Listen page.

Typed messages work even when the My voice switch is off.

With some Features turned on, the Text page also shows:
  Quick phrases ............ A row of buttons above the box: click one to say it.
  Will say ................. With "Cancel before it's spoken", a bar showing what the voice is
                             about to say, with a Cancel button.
  How to read it / Replay /  With Learning mode, under each line: how to read it in Latin letters,
  Save                       hear it again in its own language, and keep it for studying.


LISTEN PAGE
-----------

Captions of what other people say, translated. It starts switched off every time, because it
uses your ElevenLabs credits for as long as it runs and people are talking.

Listen to
  App ...................... Only one program's sound, for example VRChat.exe (Windows 11 or an
                             up-to-date Windows 10). It waits for the program to start and
                             reconnects if it restarts. Your own changed voice isn't captioned.
  Speakers ................. Everything playing on one playback device.
  Input .................... A recording device, such as a virtual cable or a second mic.
  Opens above .............. Put the marker above the game's background sound and below the
                             voices. Loud music above the marker gets transcribed too.
  Captions appear .......... Sooner: fastest, but half sentences translate worse.
                             Balanced: splits at natural pauses.
                             Fuller: waits for whole sentences, best wording.

Translation
  They speak ............... The other people's language, or "Detect automatically".
  Translate into ........... Your language. Type to search.
  Translation service ...... Same as on My voice. Use DeepL here: the free service runs out
                             after a few minutes of steady captions.
  Tell speakers apart ...... Splits a caption into "Voice 1", "Voice 2" lines when several people
                             talk. The numbers only apply inside that one caption.
  Show the original words .. Shows what was said under the translation.
  Skip speech in my language  Hides people who already speak your language.
  Translation service ...... Same choices as on My voice. Claude, Gemini or OpenAI understand
                             slang much better. Here they always translate faithfully.
  Send to VRChat chatbox ... Shows the newest caption above your avatar (people near you can read
                             it too). Needs OSC on in VRChat.
  Caption size ............. Text size of the captions.

Captions
  Captions only ............ Shrinks the window to just the captions and keeps it on top of the
                             game. To see it inside VR, pin that window with an overlay app such
                             as XSOverlay or OVR Toolkit, or just use the VR page instead.
  Show controls ............ Goes back to the full window.
  Clear .................... Empties the caption list.


VR PAGE
-------

Puts the captions on a small, see-through panel inside your headset, over any SteamVR game
(VRChat included). It is off until you flip the switch at the top right. It waits for SteamVR to
be running and lets go when SteamVR closes. Captions come from the Listen page, so turn Listen on
too.

It is built to be light: the panel is only redrawn when the words or the look change, it is
hidden completely when there is nothing to show, and the main window draws nothing while it is
minimized.

Placement
  Where .................... Head: moves with you and is always in view (the default).
                             Left hand / Right hand: on the back of your hand, look at your
                             wrist to read it.
                             Fixed: stays in one spot in the room, like a screen.
  Put it in front of me .... (Fixed) Places the panel where you are looking right now.
  Distance / Height /
  Sideways ................. (Head and Fixed) Where the panel sits, in metres.
  Size ..................... How wide the panel is.

Show
  What I say ............... Also shows your own words (in a different colour).
  Original words ........... Shows what was actually said under each translation.
  Hide after ............... Fades the panel out after this many quiet seconds (or never).
  Show who I'm talking to .. A small note at the top of the panel ("Talking to: a girl").

In VR
  CIMA Box tab in the SteamVR menu
                             Press the system button on your controller: the SteamVR menu gets a
                             CIMA Box tab with big buttons (Auto, Don't know, A guy, A girl,
                             A group). Point and click with the laser to switch who you're
                             talking to without taking the headset off.

Look
  The preview at the top shows exactly what you'll see in the headset.
  Text size, Lines ......... How big the text is and how many lines are kept.
  Panel .................... How dark the panel behind the text is. 0 = just floating text.
  Overall opacity .......... How see-through everything is.
  Glow ..................... A soft glow around the panel edge.
  Text / Accent / My lines . Colours. Click a square to pick.
  Default look ............. Puts the look back to how it started.

VR needs nothing extra: everything is inside CIMABox.exe. (Before 2.7.2 it needed a file called
openvr_api.dll next to it; that file can be deleted now.)


FEATURES PAGE
-------------

Extras, each with its own switch. They are all off until you turn them on, so CIMA Box stays
simple if you don't need them. Turning one on shows its options.

Talking
  Cancel before it's spoken  Waits a moment (you choose how long) and shows what the voice is
                             about to say. Click Cancel or press the Cancel key to stop it.
  Answer in their language . When people speak to you in another language, "Translate to"
                             switches to it. It only switches after two lines in a row in that
                             language, so one short word can't flip it.
  Languages ................ Languages I speak / Languages around me: "Detect automatically" only
                             picks from these. Short phrases are often mistaken for another
                             language; this stops that. Leave a list empty to allow all.
  Clean up my swearing ..... Tone it down (milder words, with Claude, Gemini or OpenAI) or Say
                             "beep".
  Best translations ........ Bigger AI models and twice the time limit. Better for dialects and
                             slang; a bit slower and pricier.
  Try a line ............... Type a line and see how each style (Polite, Casual, Very casual, My
                             own) says it, using your own keys.
  More hotkeys ............. Hold for my real voice (your own mic goes out while held) and Stop
                             speaking (stops the voice and drops anything waiting).
  Screen translation ....... See below.

Hearing and reading
  Hear them in my ear ...... A voice reads the translated captions in your headphones. Pick the
                             voice, where it plays and how loud. Needs Listen on, and uses
                             ElevenLabs credits for every caption.
  Captions while they talk . Words show up while someone is still speaking, then the translation
                             replaces them. Uses more ElevenLabs credits.
  Captions ................. Hide swearing (f***), sound labels ([laughs], [music]), skip music
                             and noise, big high-contrast captions, and guessing gender from
                             their words (for languages where it changes how you answer).
  Same voice, same color ... Follows who is talking from one caption to the next: "Voice 1" keeps
                             its number and color. It is careful on purpose, because a few
                             seconds of speech only say so much: a voice gets its number after
                             it has been heard a few times, there are at most six, and a line
                             it can't place (very short, or between two similar voices) gets
                             a plain grey bar instead of a guess. A voice is named once and
                             then only when the speaker changes. Similar voices can still be
                             mixed up. Right-click a line to mute that voice.
  Caption window over the game
                             A small see-through window of captions on top of your game. Games
                             must run in borderless windowed mode, not fullscreen. "Clicks go
                             through it" lets you click the game underneath: set the key first,
                             it's how you get the window back.

Handy
  Quick phrases ............ Buttons on the Text page for things you say a lot. "You type these a
                             lot" suggests lines you've sent several times.
  My word list ............. Names and words that keep coming out wrong. "It writes" is what you
                             see now (optional), "Write it as" is how it should be. Words on the
                             list are never translated.
  Learning mode ............ See the Text page.
  Keep a history ........... Saves the conversation to a text file for each day, only on this PC
                             (in the CIMA Box settings folder). Search it from here.
  Profiles ................. Save your whole setup under a name and load it in one click.
  Share a setup ............ Copy my setup makes a code to paste in Discord; Paste a setup adds a
                             friend's code as a profile. Codes never include your keys, your
                             word list, your quick phrases or anything you typed, and a friend's
                             code can't switch screen translation on for you.

Screen translation
  Translates text you can see in a game or app: chat boxes, signs, menus.

  1. Window ............ pick the game or app.
  2. Translate into .... your language (it starts as the one on the Listen page).
  3. Take a snapshot ... a preview opens with the picture. Drag over the text you want, like
                         the chat box, and press Translate. Nothing is read or sent before that.

  The translation shows next to the picture (Copy them all copies it), with the captions on the
  Listen page and in the caption window, in the conversation on the Text page and, if you like,
  on the VR panel.

  In the preview: Retake takes a new picture, Whole window clears your selection, and Watch this
  area keeps looking at that part every few seconds and translates new lines as they appear
  (switch it off with "Watch for new lines"). While it watches, a "Capturing" label shows at the
  top of the window.

  While you play: the Snapshot key, and "Translate screen" in the SteamVR menu, translate right
  away with the area you last picked, because there is no preview to click when you're in the
  game. "Translate without the preview" makes the button do the same.

  Who reads the text:
    Windows (free) ... reads on your PC. Only the translation uses tokens, as usual. It can
                       read Arabic, Bosnian, Bulgarian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional),
                       Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Greek,
                       Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese,
                       Romanian, Russian, Serbian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and
                       Turkish, and only those installed in Windows (Settings > Time & language
                       > Language). Not Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Hebrew, Ukrainian,
                       Persian or Tagalog, among others. Pick the language under "Text in the
                       picture": if Windows doesn't have it yet, CIMA Box says so and offers a
                       button that opens Windows' language settings.
    AI ............... sends the picture to Claude, Gemini or OpenAI, which read any language
                       and translate in one step. Pictures cost far more than text: one
                       snapshot can cost as much as dozens of spoken lines, and watching a chat
                       box sends a picture every time it looks. Your spending limit still
                       applies.

  IMPORTANT: never capture private or sensitive information. Anything visible in the window
  can end up in the picture: passwords, keys, login screens, bank or card details, private
  messages, emails, your real name or address, or other people's personal information. With AI
  reading, the picture is sent to that AI service and is covered by their privacy rules, not
  CIMA Box's, and can't be taken back. If you wouldn't post it publicly, don't capture it.
  CIMA Box only captures the window you picked, only when you ask (or while Watch is on), and
  never saves the pictures or writes them to the log.

  Games and anti-cheat: CIMA Box never touches a game's files or memory; it only takes a
  picture of the window, like a screenshot tool. Some competitive games have rules about
  overlay and screen-capture tools, so check them before using it there.


SETTINGS (THE GEAR)
-------------------

Click the gear at the top right; click it again (or any page name) to go back.

App language
  The language of the menus: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Italiano,
  Bahasa Indonesia, Русский, العربية, 日本語, 한국어 or 简体中文. Changes right away. Translations, captions and voices have
  their own language settings.

  Adding another language: in the "lang" folder next to CIMABox.exe there is
  _template.json.txt with every text. Copy it to a new name ending in .json (for example tr.json),
  put your language's code and name at the top (for example "tr" and "Türkçe"), write the
  translations between the quotes and restart CIMA Box. Anything you leave empty stays English.
  Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Hindi can't be used for the menus (the text would show unjoined).

About
  Version history .......... What changed in every version. After each update, a "What's new"
                             window shows the changes since the version you had.
  Save settings in ......... Where cimabox.json (your settings and keys) is kept. It saves by
                             itself a moment after every change.
    Settings folder ........ %APPDATA%\CIMA Box. Out of the way; the default.
    Documents .............. A "CIMA Box Config" folder in your Documents.
    With the program ....... Right next to CIMABox.exe, so everything stays in one folder (handy
                             on the Desktop or a USB stick). Putting a cimabox.json next to
                             CIMABox.exe does the same.
                             Switching moves the file for you.
  Open settings folder ..... Opens the folder where cimabox.json is.

Accounts
  ElevenLabs API key ....... Needed for everything. From the API keys page on elevenlabs.io.
  Load voices and models ... Fetches your voices and models from ElevenLabs.
  Claude / Gemini / OpenAI API key
                             For natural translation with slang and dialects. You only need one.
  DeepL API key ............ Textbook translation, and the backup when the AI is slow.
  Show keys ................ Shows the keys instead of dots.

Hotkeys
  Push to talk ............. The key to hold while talking (when Input is set to push to talk).
  Next "Talking to" ........ Each press goes to the next choice: Auto, Don't know, A guy,
                             A girl, A group. Works while a game has focus.
  Clear .................... Removes a hotkey.
  I am / Talking to ........ The same choices as on the My voice page.

Updates
  CIMA Box updates itself. When a new version is out, an amber "Update to ..." button appears at
  the top of the window; it opens Settings, where "Update and restart" installs it. It downloads
  the new version, checks that it really comes from the author (a digital signature) and restarts.
  Your settings and keys stay.
  Check when CIMA Box starts  Looks for a new version a few seconds after starting.
  Check for updates ........ Looks now.
  Keep CIMA Box in a normal folder (Desktop, Documents...), not in Program Files, so it can
  replace itself.


TIPS
----

- Lowest delay: Changer mode, Standard quality, a short "Pause that ends a phrase". For
  Re-speak/Translate, use v4 Turbo or Flash.
- Most natural sound: Changer mode, Stability around 0.5, Style 0, a voice described as
  conversational, or a clone of yourself made from casual talking.
- Two mics (for example a VR headset and a desk mic): turn on Match my mic, and keep the same
  distance and noise removal on both.
- To sound like a native speaker when translating: Claude, Gemini or OpenAI, Casual or Very
  casual (or My own), a "Sound like..." with a region and age, and Listen on with "Match how
  they talk to me".
- To pass as a local: Claude, Gemini or OpenAI, the right Dialect, "I am" set, and a voice (or clone)
  from that region.
- Emotion tags like [excited] and capitals for stressed words only work on v3 and v4 models.
- Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Hebrew are joined up and shown right to left. Hindi and similar
  scripts may still look a little off in the text lines; the voice itself is fine.


SPENDING LIMIT (SETTINGS > ACCOUNTS)
------------------------------------

Counted per session, from when CIMA Box starts.
  ElevenLabs credits per session .. A notice appears at the top after this many.
  AI translation per session ......... The same for Claude, Gemini and OpenAI together, in US
                                      dollars (an estimate from list prices).
  Stop talking and listening at the limit
                                      Also switches off My voice and Listen until you press OK.
Press OK on the notice to carry on: the next notice comes after another full limit. ElevenLabs
credits can only be counted if the key is allowed to read usage (User: Read).


WHAT'S LEFT (BOTTOM RIGHT)
--------------------------

A small line at the bottom of every page shows what's left for the services you use:
  ElevenLabs ... credits left this month, read from your account. If it says "not available",
                 the key was made without permission to read the account (allow User: Read).
  DeepL ........ characters left this month.
  Claude, Gemini, OpenAI  They can't report what's left on a key, so this shows what CIMA Box spent
                 in this session (counted from their answers, at list price).
It turns amber when less than a tenth is left and red when it's used up. Hover for details.


PROBLEMS
--------

If a headset or mic is unplugged, CIMA Box opens it again by itself when it's plugged back in
(within a few seconds).

Settings > About > Open log file opens cimabox.log: what went wrong and when. It never contains
your API keys, so it's safe to send to whoever is helping you. Report a problem (also in About)
opens a new report on CIMA Box's GitHub page with your version and setup filled in, and puts
"CIMA Box problem report.txt" on your Desktop (the log, without keys, anything anyone said or your
Windows user name). Say what happened, drag the file into the page and press Submit. It needs a
free GitHub account, and reports there are public.

If something stops working, first look at Settings > Service status. It shows, right in the app,
whether ElevenLabs, Claude, Gemini, OpenAI and DeepL are working. For ElevenLabs, Claude and
OpenAI that comes from their status pages. DeepL and Gemini are also asked directly whether they
answer from your PC (no key is sent), and Google's incident list is read for Gemini. It's
checked every few minutes; "Couldn't check" means your PC couldn't reach it just now. When a service you use reports problems, "ElevenLabs: problems" (or
similar) appears at the top of the window: it's on their side, so wait and try again later.
Click it to open their status page.

"Add your ElevenLabs API key first" .... Paste your key under Account.
Nothing happens when I talk ............ Check the switch at the top right is on, the right mic
                                         is picked, and the level meter moves past the marker.
The other app doesn't hear the voice ... That app's microphone must be the other end of the
                                         virtual cable ("CABLE Output").
It cuts off my last word ............... Make "Pause that ends a phrase" a bit longer.
"Free translation limit reached" ....... Switch to DeepL, Claude, Gemini or OpenAI, or wait until
                                         tomorrow.
"(Claude too slow)" in the bottom strip  That phrase was done by DeepL or Free instead. Raise
                                         the Time limit a little if it happens a lot.
VR page says "Waiting for SteamVR" ..... Start SteamVR. It connects by itself.
No CIMA Box tab in the SteamVR menu .... Turn on "CIMA Box tab in the SteamVR menu" on the VR
                                         page, and switch VR on at the top right.
"(...; standard Arabic)" in the strip .. That phrase went through DeepL or Free, which don't
                                         know dialects. Raise the Time limit a little.
"Couldn't replace the program" ......... Move the CIMA Box folder somewhere you can write to (for
                                         example Documents), then update again.
"The update's signature doesn't match"   The download wasn't the real update, so it was not
                                         installed. Try again later.
"Talking to" Auto guesses wrong ........ Set it by hand (hotkey or SteamVR tab). Voice changers
                                         and avatar voices fool the guess.
Nothing shows in the headset ........... Turn Listen on too, and check the panel isn't behind
                                         you (Head is easiest).
The chatbox shows nothing .............. Turn on OSC in VRChat.
App capture fails on Listen ............ Needs Windows 11 or an up-to-date Windows 10. Use
                                         Speakers instead.
Screen translation finds no text ....... Drag over just the text in the preview. With Windows
                                         reading, pick the text's language; if it isn't in
                                         Windows yet, use the button that appears, or AI reading.
Screen snapshot is black ............... Some games block window pictures in fullscreen. Use
                                         borderless windowed mode.
The caption window can't be clicked .... "Clicks go through it" is on: press its key.
